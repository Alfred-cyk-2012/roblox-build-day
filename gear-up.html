Gear Up

Before you can bring joy, you need to first get your account setup.

What You Need to Do

1

Download Roblox

Before anything else, you need Roblox installed on your device. Roblox is available on Windows, Mac, iOS, Android, and Xbox. Head over to the official download page and get it set up — it's free!

Download Roblox →
2

Create Your Account

Sign up for a free Roblox account using your email address, or connect through your Google, Facebook, or Apple account. Pick a unique username — this will be your builder identity in the Roblox community!

Create Account →
3

Download Roblox Studio

Roblox Studio is the sister application to Roblox — it's the powerful development tool where you actually build your games, design worlds, and script experiences. It comes free with your Roblox account and is available on Windows and Mac.

Get Roblox Studio →
4

Learn to Use Lua

Lua is the programming language that powers Roblox. It's a lightweight, easy-to-learn scripting language used to make things happen in your games — from player movement to game rules, scoring systems, and interactive objects. Don't worry if you've never coded before; Lua is beginner-friendly and there are tons of tutorials available!
