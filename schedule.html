The Schedule

24 hours. One mission. Here's exactly how the day unfolds — so you can plan every minute.

9:00 AM

Doors Open — Check-In

Arrive, grab your badge, settle in, and meet your fellow builders. Grab snacks and find your workspace.

Logistics
10:00 AM

Opening Ceremony

Welcome speech, overview of the rules, introduction of mentors, and the big theme reveal. This is where the excitement begins!

Ceremony
10:30 AM

Team Formation Window

Finalize your team or find teammates. Lock in your team name and register on the portal. Solo builders confirm their solo entry.

Team
11:00 AM

🚀 BUILD STARTS

The clock is ticking! Open Roblox Studio and start building. Mentors are available for questions. Focus, create, and have fun!

Build Phase
1:00 PM

Lunch Break

Refuel! Take a 30-minute break, eat lunch, network with other teams, and come back refreshed.

Break
3:00 PM

Mentor Check-In

Mentors visit each team for a casual progress check. Get feedback, ask questions, and course-correct early.

Mentorship
6:00 PM

Dinner Break

Another break to eat, stretch, and recharge. Share what you've built so far with other teams!

Break
9:00 PM

Midnight Snack & Energy Boost

Snacks, drinks, and a quick mini-game break to keep energy up. The home stretch is coming!

Break
10:00 AM+1

Final Mentor Round

Last chance to get mentor feedback. Polish your game, fix critical bugs, and prepare for submission.

Mentorship
11:00 AM+1

⏰ BUILD ENDS — Submit!

Hands off! All projects must be submitted by this time. Late submissions will not be accepted. Push your final build to the portal.

Deadline
11:30 AM+1

Playtesting & Showcase

Everyone plays each other's games! Walk around, try other teams' creations, and leave feedback. This is the fun part.

Showcase
12:30 PM+1

🏆 Awards Ceremony

Winners announced! Prizes awarded for Best Game, Most Creative, Best Teamwork, People's Choice, and more. Celebrate what you built!

Ceremony
