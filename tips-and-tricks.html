Tips and Tricks

Shortcuts that save you plenty of time — enough for the 24 hour period!

  1. 1

    Name things by what they mean, not what they are

  2. 2

    Booleans should read as questions — e.g. isAlive, hasKey

  3. 3

    Functions should be verbs, variables should be nouns

  4. 4

    Use consistent casing conventions — camelCase for variables, PascalCase for modules

  5. 5

    Prefix private/internal things with an underscore — _internalState

  6. 6

    Use and/or instead of if when it reads naturally

  7. 7

    Prefer early returns over deep nesting

  8. 8

    Write guard clauses at the top of functions

  9. 9

    Use pairs/ipairs semantically, not interchangeably

  10. 10

    Prefer local functions over global ones

  11. 11

    Comment why, not what

  12. 12

    Use block comments to disable code, line comments for notes

  13. 13

    Document function contracts above the function

  14. 14

    Use TODO and FIXME tags to mark work left behind

  15. 15

    One idea per line, one purpose per function

  16. 16

    Blank lines separate concepts, not statements

  17. 17

    Order things: constants, services, helpers, main logic

  18. 18

    Keep lines short — 80 to 100 characters

  19. 19

    Optimize for the reader, not the writer

  20. 20

    Consistency beats preference
