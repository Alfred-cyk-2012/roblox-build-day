FAQ

Got questions? We've got answers. If you don't see yours here, reach out on Discord!

Anyone can participate! Whether you're a complete beginner or an experienced developer, Roblox Build Day is for you. All you need is a Roblox account and Roblox Studio installed. There are no age restrictions, but participants under 13 should have parental supervision.

Yes! Roblox Build Day is completely free to enter. Roblox and Roblox Studio are both free tools. The only cost is your time and creativity.

Teams can have 1 to 4 members. We recommend 2–3 for the best balance of productivity and coordination. Solo entries are also welcome and completely valid!

No worries! Join our Discord server before the event and use the #looking-for-team channel. We'll help match you with other solo builders who have complementary skills. You can also form teams on the day itself during the Team Formation Window.

It helps, but it's not required! If you're a builder or designer, you can team up with a scripter. We also provide tutorials and mentors to help beginners get started with Lua during the event. Check out our Tutorials page to get a head start!

You can build any type of Roblox experience — an obby, a tycoon, a story game, a simulator, etc. The only restrictions are: it must be built from scratch during the 24 hours (no pre-made assets or code), it must follow Roblox community standards, and it must be playable by judges when time is up.

Yes! Roblox Toolbox assets (free models, sounds, images) are allowed. However, you cannot use pre-written scripts from the toolbox — all code must be written by your team during the build period. Custom-made assets are always encouraged over toolbox items.

Projects are judged by a panel of experienced Roblox developers and community figures based on four criteria: Creativity (originality of concept), Execution (how well it's built and scripted), Fun Factor (how enjoyable it is to play), and Polish (UI, sound, overall feel). There's also a People's Choice award voted on by all participants.

The total prize pool is $5,000 distributed across multiple categories: 1st Place ($2,000), 2nd Place ($1,000), 3rd Place ($500), Most Creative ($500), People's Choice ($500), and Best Solo Builder ($500). All participants also receive an exclusive Roblox Build Day badge!

If it's an in-person event, we encourage you to rest when needed — there will be quiet zones available. If it's virtual, manage your time however you like! The key is that your game must be submitted and playable by the deadline. Team members can take shifts if needed.

That's okay! Submit whatever you have by the deadline. A working prototype with a core gameplay loop is much better than an ambitious game that crashes. Judges appreciate polish and completeness over scope. You can always keep working on it after the event!

Mentors are available during scheduled check-ins (typically every 3–4 hours) and are on-call via Discord for urgent questions throughout the build. They can help with debugging, design advice, and pointing you to the right documentation — but they won't write code for you!
