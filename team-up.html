Team Up

Great games aren't built alone. Find your squad, combine your skills, and create something extraordinary together.

Why Team Up?

24 hours is tight. When you team up, you divide the work and multiply the creativity. Builders who team up ship 3x more features and have way more fun doing it.

How to Form Your Team

👥

Bring Your Friends

Already know who you want to build with? Register as a team during sign-up. Teams can be 1–4 people. Just make sure everyone has their account ready!

Easiest
🔍

Find Teammates

Solo but don't want to be? Join our matchmaking channel on Discord and get paired with builders who have complementary skills — scripters, builders, UI designers, and more.

Popular
🐺

Go Solo

Some of the best games are solo creations. If you're confident you can handle all roles — building, scripting, testing — go it alone and prove what one person can do!

What Skills Are Needed?

📝 Game Design

Planning mechanics, levels, and player experience

🏗️ Building

Creating 3D environments, maps, and structures in Studio

💻 Scripting (Lua)

Coding game logic, systems, and interactions

🎨 UI/UX Design

Menus, HUDs, icons, and player-facing interfaces

🎵 Audio & SFX

Sound effects, background music, and audio cues

🧪 Testing & QA

Finding bugs, balancing gameplay, and polishing
